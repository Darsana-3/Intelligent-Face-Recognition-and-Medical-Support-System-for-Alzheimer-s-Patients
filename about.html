About Us

Reimagining care for cognitive health

Alzheimer's Support blends modern AI with structured medical workflows to give Alzheimer's patients dignity, independence, and safety.

Our Mission

Empowering memory through intelligence

Alzheimer's disease affects millions worldwide. Patients struggle to recognize familiar faces, remember medications, and maintain daily routines. Caregivers carry an immense load.

Alzheimer's Support addresses these challenges by integrating VGG-Face deep learning for visitor identification with a structured platform for medical management — connecting patients, doctors, and caregivers in one secure ecosystem.

AI-First

Embedding-based recognition without retraining.

Patient-Centric

Designed for cognitive accessibility.

Secure

Role-based access with hashed credentials.

Scalable

Built on Django for production deployment.

How It Works

Three layers, one ecosystem

Enrollment

Caregivers upload 3-5 photos of family and friends. The system extracts 512-dimensional facial embeddings using VGG-Face and stores them securely.

Recognition

When a visitor arrives, the system captures their face, computes cosine similarity with stored embeddings, and announces who they are.

Care

Meanwhile, doctors prescribe medications, schedule appointments, and track adherence — all reaching the patient via timely reminders.
