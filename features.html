Platform Features

Complete toolkit for Alzheimer's care

From visitor identification to medication adherence, every feature is purpose-built for cognitive wellness.

Face Enrollment

Upload multiple photos per person — son, daughter, spouse, doctor, caregiver. The system extracts averaged embeddings for robust recognition.

Real-Time Recognition

Webcam-based recognition with cosine similarity matching. Returns name, relationship, and confidence score in seconds.

Voice Announcement

Browser text-to-speech announces "This is your daughter, Priya" aloud — providing reassurance and reducing confusion.

Smart Medicine Reminders

Doctor-prescribed plus self-added medications. Set frequency, timing, dosage, and duration. Mark as taken to log adherence.

Appointment System

Doctors schedule consultations with patients. Status tracking — scheduled, completed, cancelled, rescheduled.

Prescriptions

Doctors issue digital prescriptions with diagnosis, multiple medicines, and instructions. Patients receive notifications.

Emergency Contacts

Add next-of-kin with one-tap visibility. Designate a primary contact for critical situations.

Custom Alerts

Hydration, exercise, daily routines, or anything else. Patients and caregivers set their own reminder schedules.

Approval Workflow

Patients request medicine refills or treatment changes. Doctors review, approve or reject with comments.

Admin Oversight

System-wide visibility for administrators — manage doctors, view patient profiles, audit activity logs.

Medical History

Track Alzheimer's stage, chronic conditions, allergies, surgeries, and family history for informed care decisions.

In-App Notifications

Real-time notifications for prescriptions, appointments, alerts, and approval responses across all roles.
